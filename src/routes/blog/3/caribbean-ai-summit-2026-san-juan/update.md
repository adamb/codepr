In October the Caribbean’s biggest in-person AI conference lands in San Juan. Caribbean AI Summit 2026 (organized with the Puerto Rico AI Community, PRAIC) runs Friday, October 9–Saturday, October 10 at the Puerto Rico Convention Center (doors about 8:45am; program roughly 9am–6pm).

Holberton Coding School Puerto Rico is proud to be a sponsor!  We'll be there with a booth and some fun AI Demos!
We're also provding volunteers from our students and grads.  

This is not a spectator sport. The useful question is simple: what are you going to make?

What’s on the calendar
Two full days of talks and tracks — business, technical, and learning — plus the hallway conversations that actually move projects. Official details live at caribbeansummit.ai. Tickets are on Eventbrite (and linked from the site). Check live prices before you buy — public tiers have moved during the year.

Summit: Fri–Sat, Oct 9–10, Puerto Rico Convention Center, San Juan.
Hackathon: “AI for Puerto Rico,” during the Summit, in person.
Prizes: $10,000 total — 1st $5,000 · 2nd $3,000 · 3rd $2,000.
Ticket: a Summit ticket is required to hack; join the hackathon waitlist after you have one.
Teams: small teams (check the live hackathon page for current size rules). Solo sign-ups can still join the waitlist.
The hackathon is the point for builders
If you only have bandwidth for one track, make it the build. Mentors on the floor. Judging on what you ship for Puerto Rico — not a slide deck about what you might ship later. What to do this week
Buy a Summit ticket via caribbeansummit.ai or Eventbrite — confirm the live price.
If you’re hacking, join the hackathon waitlist as soon as you have the ticket.
Sketch one problem in Puerto Rico you’d actually build for. Healthcare ops, small-business tooling, civic data, language, logistics — pick something concrete.
Tell one other builder. Show up with a teammate if you can.
We’ll post again after the event with what Holberton shipped and who you should know. Until then: get the ticket, find your teammates, and show up ready to build.
