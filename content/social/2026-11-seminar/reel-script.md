# Reel script (30 to 40 seconds, vertical)

Status: draft. Filmed by Adam on a phone, or a screen recording with voiceover.

| Time | On screen | Voiceover / text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Phone showing an Instagram post with comments | "Every one of these comments is a potential customer." |
| 3–8s | Someone types a comment, a DM pops up | "Watch. Someone comments a keyword, and they get a DM instantly." |
| 8–15s | Tap the link, short form fills in on the phone | "The DM sends them to a short form. Name, email, WhatsApp. Ten seconds." |
| 15–23s | Screen recording: the new lead appears in Odoo | "And it lands right here in our CRM, tagged and ready for follow-up. Nobody copied anything." |
| 23–30s | Adam to camera (or text card) | "We built this with free tools, and we're teaching it in a free seminar." |
| 30–35s | Text card: date, format, keyword | "Comment SEMINAR and I'll send you a spot." |

**On-screen text hook (first frame):** "Stop losing leads in your DMs"

**Caption:** short version of the post caption, ending with "Comment SEMINAR for a free spot."

**Best demo:** do the flow for real on camera once the test pipeline works, so the Reel is the demo. Use a test contact, never a real lead's data.
