# Seminar outline (90 minutes)

Status: draft for Adam.

**Title:** From Instagram comment to CRM lead: build your own free lead pipeline

1. **Why this matters (10 min).** Leads are already in your comments and DMs. Live example: the signup flow everyone in the room just used.
2. **The free toolkit (10 min).** Instagram Business account, ManyChat free plan (and its limits: 25 contacts a month, 4 automations), a web form, n8n, Odoo.
3. **Live build, part 1: comment to DM (15 min).** Set up a keyword trigger and DM in ManyChat.
4. **Live build, part 2: DM to form (10 min).** A short form with the service filled in from the link.
5. **Live build, part 3: form to CRM (15 min).** n8n receives the form and creates a tagged lead in Odoo.
6. **Content that gets comments (10 min).** Hooks, keyword calls to action, one offer per post.
7. **Q&A (20 min).** Close with: "Want us to build this for you? That's what Code PR does."
